## Quick Summary

A final year project objective is not a list of activities such as buying components, writing code or completing a report. It describes an outcome that will be achieved and evaluated. In an electronics or IoT project, each objective should connect one requirement to a system output and a method of verification.

Good objectives control scope. If an objective cannot be matched to a test case, metric or piece of evidence, it is probably too broad. Use verbs such as design, develop, measure, compare and evaluate, then state the function or parameter that genuinely belongs to the project.

## Separate aim, objective and feature

The aim is the overall purpose, such as developing a prototype for monitoring a storage room. Objectives divide that purpose into assessable outcomes. Features are system functions such as alerts or CSV export. A feature should become an objective only when it is important to the problem and will be tested.

Avoid phrases such as efficient smart system unless efficient and smart have operational definitions. Replace them with a specific outcome, such as recording temperature at a defined interval and issuing an alert when a tested threshold is exceeded.

## Use a testable objective structure

A useful structure is verb + technical outcome + operating condition or scope + evaluation method. For example: design an ESP32 node that records temperature and humidity every 60 seconds for evaluation of data loss during a 24-hour test. Numerical targets must come from requirements or justification rather than being invented to sound scientific.

Do not promise a particular accuracy before selecting a reference and calibration procedure. When uncertainty remains, write an objective to evaluate error or repeatability under stated conditions.

## Trace objectives through the report

Every objective should reappear in the methodology and results. Build a traceability table with columns for objective, module, test case, evidence and result location. If an objective mentions response time, Chapter 3 must explain how time is measured and Chapter 4 must show the data rather than only an application screenshot.

Three or four focused objectives are often easier to defend than a long feature list. Follow the institution's required format, but keep every objective meaningful.

## Improve weak objective statements

A weak statement is "to use IoT in agriculture." A stronger version is "to develop an ESP32 prototype that records soil-moisture readings and pump status for two test zones." A claim such as reducing water waste is not defensible unless water use is measured against a baseline.

Use words such as detect, prevent and optimise only when the project defines the event, comparison and metric. Objectives are engineering commitments, not marketing headlines.

## Validation Plan

- Map every objective to at least one test case.
- Check that words such as fast, accurate and efficient have metrics.
- Confirm the required data can be collected with available tools.
- Ask another reader to explain each objective without the feature list.

Run tests with one controlled change at a time. Preserve configuration, raw measurements and failed runs rather than only successful screenshots. For a final year project, repeatable evidence is stronger than promotional claims. When the available instrument or method cannot prove a statement, report that limitation and define the additional experiment that would be required.

## Common Mistakes

- Writing development activities as research objectives.
- Using smart without a defined function.
- Promising social impact that is not measured.
- Leaving an objective unanswered in the results chapter.

Move these risks into a checklist or project risk register before demo day. A fault found while architecture and wiring are still flexible is normally less expensive to correct than one discovered after hardware, firmware and report chapters depend on the same assumption.

## FAQ

### How many objectives should an FYP have?

There is no universal number. Three or four focused objectives are often manageable, but students should follow their institution's format and ensure every objective receives evidence.

### Can an objective name a component?

Yes when the technology is central to scope, but the outcome should remain the focus. Avoid locking a part too early when the requirement is not stable.

- For **Improve weak objective statements**, what was design decision 4, which alternative was rejected, and what evidence supports the choice?

A strong answer should cite a requirement, primary datasheet, calculation or result from the student's own tests. Explain the selected option, the rejected alternative, test conditions and remaining limitation. Honest boundaries show engineering judgement and make future improvements credible.
